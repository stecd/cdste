SafeCup
Role:ideation, research, Raspberry-Pi development, electronics, prototyping
Process Documentation
SafeCup is an interactive lid that keeps your drink safe while out. Spend less time worrying and more time enjoying yourself. Using Google Cloud Vision APIs we installed a little camera on a removable pint lid that scans and stores your driver's licence and then allows you to scan your own face to lock and unlock your cup. This project attempts to provide a feeling of safety when out at a bar, and the freedom to go dance or use the restroom and leave your drink unattended.
