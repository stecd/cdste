About
Hey! My name is Stephanie Claudino Daffara, but I go by Ste. I am currently in my last semester at UCBerkeley studying Computer Science. The classes I take focus on Graphics and Machine learning; the research projects I participate in involve Data Science and Visualization; and the collaborations I do with professors involve Augmented Reality and Art. For fun I lead an Immersive Cinema Research and Production group.
In the same way that I practice furthering my own knowledge, I actively seek projects and research teams that focus on the intersection of technology and education pushing for a reality where everyone has easy access to education and the mental, emotional and financial freedom to follow their creative passions.
So if you are looking for a hand in software development, design or immersive experiences lets talk:stephaniecd@berkeley.edu
